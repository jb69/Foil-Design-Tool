# Foilborne

Foilborne records your hydrofoil sessions on your Apple Watch and shows them on your iPhone: where you went, how fast, when the board was flying, and what carried it. Recording is free in every discipline. One discipline's analysis is free, and each of the others is a module you can buy. The disciplines are prone, SUP, wing, kite, parawing, pump, tow, surf and eFoil.

By Jason Bird (JB69).

## Support

For questions, problems or ideas, email foilborne.app@gmail.com or open an issue at <https://github.com/jb69/Foil-Design-Tool/issues>. If something went wrong, the app can send its logs with your question: on the iPhone, open Settings, then Diagnostics, then *Share diagnostics*.

- **Restoring purchases:** on the iPhone, open Disciplines and tap *Restore purchases*.
- **Offer codes:** on the iPhone, open Disciplines and tap *Redeem a code*.

## Privacy

Your sessions stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch; there is no account and no analytics. Read the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
